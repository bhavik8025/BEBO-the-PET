## LinkedIn Post

🤖 BEBO the PET just got a major upgrade — and if you have an older version, this is time-sensitive. Read on.

For anyone who hasn't met BEBO yet: it's a free, open-source AI assistant that lives right on your Windows desktop — a tiny animated robot, always on top, always one click away. No browser tabs, no waiting, no subscription. Paste text, pick an action, get your answer in under 2 seconds.

BEBO 2.0.1 just shipped with a big list of upgrades:
🧠 New, faster AI brain (GPT-OSS 120B via Groq)
🎨 Color-coded action buttons
🌗 Dark / light theme toggle
🎙️ Voice input — press Win+H and speak
⌨️ Fully remappable keyboard shortcuts
🖥️ Multi-monitor support
🔔 In-app update alerts, so you never miss a release again

Now the important part — if you downloaded BEBO before 15th July 2026:

Your version runs on an AI model (Llama 3.3 70B) that Groq is retiring on August 16, 2026. This isn't a BEBO bug — Groq is sunsetting this model for every app built on it, not just mine. But after Aug 16, your BEBO's AI buttons will stop responding unless you update.

Good news: updating takes under a minute, and your saved API key carries over automatically. No re-setup, no new account.

Steps in the image below 👇

Still 100% free. Still open source. Still just one click away — whether you're updating or trying BEBO for the first time.
